About

Jhared Tula is a filmmaker based in Chattanooga, TN. His work sits at the intersection of narrative and documentary — drawn to moments that refuse to be resolved.

As a director, editor, and producer, he has shaped projects from first idea to final cut. He believes the edit is where a film's soul is found.

His work has been recognized at film festivals and student showcases across the American South.
